- Supports Minecraft 1.21.5 through 26.3 (Fabric)
- Suggests the names of players you've messaged or partied with, not only your guild members
- Remembered names are saved between sessions with their proper Minecraft capitalization
- New /delname command to remove a name from the suggestions
- Name suggestions for many more party commands (invite, kick, promote, remove, accept and more)
- Fixed names you message with /msg, /tell, /w, /t, /whisper or /boop not being remembered
- Names are only remembered from messages you send on Hypixel; nothing is recorded on other servers or in singleplayer
